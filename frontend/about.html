About Meridian General Hospital

A multi-speciality hospital built around clear communication, safe practice and respect for every patient.

A hospital that listens first

Meridian General Hospital was founded to make good medical care easier to reach. Our 250-bed campus houses eight clinical departments, a 24-hour emergency centre, modern operation theatres and an on-site diagnostic laboratory.

Every patient has a named consultant, a clear care plan and access to their own consultation notes and prescriptions through the patient portal.

Our mission

To deliver safe, compassionate and affordable care, and to treat every patient with dignity.

Our vision

To be the most trusted community hospital in western India, known for clinical quality and kindness.
